# Article companion films
Workflow: faceless-explainer. Mode: automation from approved blog implementation.
Destination: GitHub Pages article video panels. Four 24-second, 16:9 Chinese caption films.
Scope: summaries of the four existing demo articles, explicitly labeled 示例稿 / 字幕概览.
Narration: none. Music: none. Full text remains in the reading panel; device TTS reads the full article.
Visual direction: paper #f8f8f7, graphite #30302e, sage #5f705f, original typographic diagrams. No copied reference assets.
Motion: smooth settling card entrances, line draw and linear reading progress. No bounce, autoplay or rapid flashing.
Story: three ideas per article, eight seconds per idea, consistent title and three-step marker.
Output: native MP4 H.264, 1280×720, 24fps; matching local posters.
Source: ../../src/content/posts/*.md. All summaries are editorial paraphrases of these demo articles.
